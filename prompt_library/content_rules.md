# Buffago Content Rules
Prompt Version: prompt-library-v1

## Allowed Content
- Restaurant discovery
- Wing rankings
- Community
- Challenges
- Memes
- Travel
- Game mechanics
- XP
- Badges
- Crawls
- Leaderboards
- Statistics
- Food holidays
- Current events
- Sports tie-ins
- Seasonal content

## Forbidden Content
- Politics
- Religion
- NSFW
- Offensive humor
- Attacking restaurants
- Shaming users
- Medical advice
- Financial advice
- Anything controversial

## Content Rules
- Keep the tone fun first.
- Keep restaurant coverage respectful.
- Do not invent negative claims about a restaurant.
- Do not shame users for their preferences or rankings.
- If current events are used, they must stay light, timely, and non-controversial.
- Sports tie-ins should stay playful and local to the food angle.
- Travel content should emphasize discovery, not status signaling.
