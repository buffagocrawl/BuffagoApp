# Shareable Food Post Rules
Prompt Version: prompt-library-v2

## Goal
Create food posts that feel sendable, taggable, comment-worthy, and instantly social.

## Core Rule
- Every post should feel like something a user would send to a friend, tag someone in, debate in the comments, or use to make wing plans.

## Use These Angles
- Tag a friend
- Send this to someone
- Settle flats versus drums
- Debate sauce, ranch versus blue cheese, or heat level
- Make plans to get wings
- Challenge someone
- Call out a wing-loving friend

## Ban These Patterns
- "If this wing had a voicemail..."
- "understood the assignment"
- Wings, sauce, or plates acting like humans
- Random surreal jokes
- Overly clever one-liners
- Captions that do not match the image text
- POV unless it is clearly strong and literal

## Image Text Rules
- Prefer 3 to 8 words
- Prefer 1 to 2 short lines
- Readable in under 2 seconds
- No long sentences
- No abstract jokes
- No wing personality concepts

## Caption Rules
- Keep it short
- Keep it direct
- Push tag, send, share, comment, like, reply, vote, choose, or plan behavior
- Match the image text angle exactly
- Never publish a caption that only describes the wings without asking for an action

## Working Examples
- Image text: SEND THIS TO YOUR WING CREW
  Caption: You know who needs to see this.
- Image text: IF THEY DON'T REPLY IN 10 MINUTES / THEY OWE YOU WINGS
  Caption: Send this and start the timer.
- Image text: WHO'S EATING THIS WITH YOU?
  Caption: Tag the friend who's never saying no.
- Image text: FIRST @ BUYS THE WINGS
  Caption: Don't make the rules.
- Image text: FLATS OR DRUMS? / PICK A SIDE
  Caption: Settle it in the comments.
- Image text: CANCEL YOUR PLANS. / GET WINGS.
  Caption: This is your sign.
