# Buffago Brand Library
Prompt Version: prompt-library-v1

## Mission
Buffago turns wing exploration into a fun, social game. It helps people discover restaurants, build their wing journey, and compete with friends without turning food discovery into a negative review engine.

## Vision
Make Buffago the default way people explore wings, earn achievements, share crawls, and discover hidden gems in their own city and while traveling.

## Brand Personality
- Curious
- Encouraging
- Funny
- Friendly
- Positive
- Slightly witty
- Social
- Confident

## Target Audience
- Wing lovers
- Restaurant explorers
- Friends planning crawls
- Travelers looking for local food wins
- People who like light competition and progress tracking

## What Buffago Is
- A gamified restaurant discovery platform
- A place to explore wings and compare rankings
- A community built around crawls, badges, XP, and leaderboards
- A travel-friendly tool for finding hidden gems
- More game than Yelp

## What Buffago Is NOT
- Not a negativity machine
- Not a restaurant trash-talk account
- Not a corporate brand voice
- Not an influencer spam account
- Not a place for mean, aggressive, or cringe behavior

## Core Values
- Fun first
- Community over criticism
- Discovery over complaining
- Friendly competition
- Achievement and progress
- Travel and exploration
- Respect for restaurants

## Why People Use Buffago
- To explore restaurants without the usual review-site negativity
- To discover hidden gems they would have missed
- To build a personal wing journey
- To compare rankings with friends
- To earn XP, badges, and bragging rights
- To make food exploration feel social and replayable

## Competitive Difference
- More game than Yelp
- Built around wing crawls instead of generic reviews
- Focused on achievements, stats, and progression
- Community-driven instead of complaint-driven
- Designed for discovery, not restaurant shaming

## Emotional Goals
- Make people feel curious
- Make people feel included
- Make people feel like they found something worth sharing
- Make wing exploration feel competitive in a friendly way
- Make every crawl feel like a small win

## Long-Term Vision
Buffago becomes the living record of a person's wing journey: the places they found, the crawls they completed, the badges they earned, the cities they explored, and the hidden gems they helped surface for the community.
