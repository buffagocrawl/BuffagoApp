# Buffaverse advisory review

Current inferred meaning: an opt-in legendary/progression/event area. First action, progress, reward, and connection to wing ratings are not self-evident from current evidence; the name is branded but needs a one-line explanation. Panel consensus: distracting if early; potentially valuable later.

| Direction | Benefit / effort / risk | Beta suitability / support / decision |
|---|---|---|
| A. Clarify current | Add one sentence, first action, progress and rating connection; small; low risk | internal beta; broad support; **only if surfaced** |
| B. Progressive unlock | Protect activation until milestone; small/medium; discovery risk | before beta; strongest support; **recommend** |
| C. Umbrella layer | Could consolidate Passport/missions/achievements; large; taxonomy/nav risk | post-beta; mixed support; **defer** |

Smallest useful change: “Buffaverse unlocks legendary wing challenges as you rate and explore.” Do not add functionality during discovery.
