# Simplification recommendations

## Keep prominent

Discovery/Wingdex, restaurant detail, simple rating, direct reward, a single Home next action, and useful mascot feedback.

## Reveal progressively

XP explanation, streaks, missions, Passport, badges/titles, Crawls/Wing Battle, notifications, friends/leaderboards, referrals, and Buffaverse.

## Consider delaying or removing

Early Buffaverse promotion; referral qualification presentation; multiple simultaneous Home cards; empty social surfaces; any new currency or social feature. These compete with the rating loop, require users/density, or lack evidence. Serrano disposition: challenge accumulation, keep feature flags safe, and test real demand before expanding.
