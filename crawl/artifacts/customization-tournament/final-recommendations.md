### BuffaGo Wingman

**Promise:** Your wing story becomes visible. Give every user the existing Wingman after onboarding. A verified rating, crawl, mission, state or milestone earns a small curated item; the user can equip it on profile and contextual moments. The primary loop is explore -> verify -> earn -> equip -> share. The secondary loop is weekly mission, travel and collection progress. No friends are required.

- **New user:** default mascot plus a first-rating item. **Casual:** may ignore equipment without losing function. **Power user:** permanent travel/crawl prestige.
- **Configuration:** one frame/nameplate plus up to three mascot slots at launch. Show on profile hero, mission completion, leaderboard row, crawl recap and share card; not continuous navigation/map/rating decoration.
- **Taxonomy:** launch shirt, hat, accessory, background, frame, nameplate/title; defer body variants, shoes, handhelds, extra archetypes, animation.
- **Earn / rarity:** verified missions, ratings, crawls, geography and transparent events. Use provenance-first (“earned by...”) with Local/Seasonal/Founder/Challenge Champion as filters, not a scarcity ladder. Achievement ownership is permanent; seasonal visibility archives. No duplicate currency.
- **Accessibility/safety:** text provenance, non-color signals, high contrast, static reduced-motion reveal, curated layers, no uploads/free text/trade/paid advantage, opt-out public loadout, no exact location cosmetics.
- **Analytics/flags:** catalog viewed, earn, reveal, equip, profile impression, share, qualified action, motion setting; gate each phase and retain a holdout.
