# Legendary copy hierarchy

1. Eyebrow: `LEGENDARY RIGHT NOW`
2. Restaurant identity: the largest text in the experience.
3. Reason: one human sentence explaining why this stop is special.
4. Countdown: a compact, honest remaining-window label.
5. Mission: one sentence, one action — `Rate wings at [restaurant] before the window ends.`
6. Outcome: `Legendary stop complete` and `Recognition recorded · reward reference pending`.

Removed from user-facing copy: event terminology, participation language, internal reward mechanics, vague hype, competing primary CTAs, and unsupported popularity claims. “Not sponsored” is retained as secondary trust copy.
